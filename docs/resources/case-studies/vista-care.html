How Vista Care reduced med errors, boosted staff accountability and gained full organisational visibility.

ORGANIZATIONVista Care Inc.
SETTINGI/DD, developmental and mental health
STATESWisconsin, Illinois, Colorado, Utah, Nevada, South Dakota
USINGeMAR+ and MedBox
LIFE BEFORE IMPRUVON

A digital copy of a paper MAR is still a paper MAR.

Vista Care largely mirrored paper MAR workflows in a digital format. Medication processes varied by region and pharmacy integration, resulting in limited standardisation and minimal built-in safeguards.

The system lacked a true workflow, making it difficult to distinguish documentation gaps from actual medication errors. Limited visibility into medication accuracy made oversight reactive rather than proactive.

Initials were the only proof of administration, with few safeguards against wrong dose, wrong time or wrong person.
Performance could not be compared across states, so there was no meaningful, actionable data.
Vista Care could not quantify its own medication error rate, or confidently tell the story of medication accuracy.
IMPLEMENTATION

Region by region, software before hardware.

1

Adoption-first phased rollout

Impruvon went in region by region, letting Vista Care build confidence before scaling across states. eMAR+ launched first and MedBoxes followed later, to reduce operational strain and support smoother DSP adoption.

2

Hands-on support

Dedicated Impruvon team members ran onboarding, training and weekly check-ins, which helped separate user error from system issues and refine the workflows.

3

Diverse workforce enablement

Training was built for a wide range of users, from new DSPs to experienced staff, so it worked across a highly varied workforce.

Accountability you can trace.

Transferring responsibility is simple now. The Impruvon system creates clear accountability for staff responsible for medication administration. In the previous system, Vista Care could not distinguish whether someone logged in to administer medication, write a note or complete another task.

With Impruvon there is stronger quality assurance oversight, and the system ensures staff complete the required three medication checks every time.

“Operational excellence is hard to achieve, but Impruvon has gotten us one step closer. By making medication administration compliant by design, they have taken the guesswork out of the hands of our staff.”

Kelli Anderson, Executive Director of Illinois, Vista Care Inc.
