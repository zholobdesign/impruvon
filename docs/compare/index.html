Not another everything-platform. The medication-safety specialist.

All-in-one EHRs treat medication as one module and stay software-only. Impruvon is purpose-built for I/DD residential care, specialises completely in medication safety, and is the only platform with hardware.

Where the difference actually shows up

Competitors are everything-platforms: medication is one module among many. That is a reasonable choice if your problem is 'we need one system for everything'.

It is the wrong choice if your problem is 'a med error can seriously harm someone who can't advocate for themselves, and my staff are non-clinical and stretched thin'.
